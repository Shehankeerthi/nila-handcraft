Returns & Refunds Policy

Not quite right? Here's how returns, exchanges and refunds work.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Return Eligibility

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging, and in the same condition you received it. Because every piece is handmade, natural variation in colour and grain is expected and isn't grounds for return on its own.

How to Start a Return

Email hello@nilahandcrafts.lk with your order number and the reason for the return. We'll confirm eligibility and send you return instructions within 2 business days.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we'll process your refund to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. You'll receive an email confirming the refund.

Exchanges

Prefer a different size or piece? Let us know when you request your return and we'll arrange an exchange, subject to stock availability.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked as final sale or clearance
  • Custom or made-to-order pieces
  • Items showing signs of use beyond inspection

Who Pays Return Shipping

If the return is due to our error (wrong or damaged item), we cover return shipping. For change-of-mind returns, return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

Contact Us

Questions about a return? Reach us at hello@nilahandcrafts.lk or visit our Contact page.
