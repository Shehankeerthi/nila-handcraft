Shipping Policy

Where we deliver, how long it takes, and what it costs.

On this page Delivery Areas Processing & Delivery Times Shipping Costs Order Tracking Delays Damaged in Transit Contact Us

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver island-wide across Sri Lanka. International shipping isn't available yet, but let us know if you'd like it — we're evaluating international couriers.

Processing & Delivery Times

  • Order processing: 1–2 business days, since most pieces are packed to order.
  • Colombo & suburbs: 2–3 business days after dispatch.
  • Rest of Sri Lanka: 3–5 business days after dispatch.

Shipping Costs

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on package weight and delivery location. We occasionally offer free island-wide delivery over a minimum order value — any active promotion is shown at checkout.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, we'll email you a tracking number and courier details so you can follow its progress.

Delays

During public holidays or peak seasons, processing and delivery can take a little longer than usual. We'll let you know if your order is affected.

Damaged in Transit

Please inspect your order on arrival. If anything is damaged in transit, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we'll arrange a replacement or refund — see our Returns & Refunds Policy for details.

Contact Us

Questions about a delivery? Reach us at hello@nilahandcrafts.lk or visit our Contact page.
