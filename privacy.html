Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or contact us, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and the order details you provide. If you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address.

How We Use It

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders, and communicate with you about them
  • Respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates and, where you've opted in, occasional newsletter emails
  • Improve our products, website and customer service
  • Meet legal and accounting obligations

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers needed to run our store — for example, delivery couriers (to fulfil shipping) and, once a payment gateway is integrated, our payment processor (to process transactions) — and only to the extent needed for those services.

Payment Data

We do not store your full card details on our servers. Card payments are handled directly by our payment processor, which is certified to the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser storage to remember items in your cart and keep you signed in between visits. We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate details, or request that we delete your account and associated data, subject to any records we're required to keep for legal or accounting purposes. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Data Retention

We retain order records for as long as required by applicable tax and consumer-protection law, and account information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services.

Children's Privacy

Our store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@nilahandcrafts.lk or visit our Contact page.
