HOW IT'S MADE

From Coconut Shell to Keepsake

01

Select

Mature coconut shells are collected and sorted by shape, thickness and natural grain.

02

Clean & Cut

Shells are cleaned, cut and shaped into bowls, beads or panels ready for carving.

03

Carve & Sand

Artisans hand-carve details and sand every surface smooth, piece by piece.

04

Polish & Finish

A natural oil finish brings out the shell's grain before careful packing and delivery.
